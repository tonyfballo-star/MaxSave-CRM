# AgencyZoom review vs MaxSaveHub (2026-10-02)

Source: signed-in walkthrough of app.agencyzoom.com (trial account, 18% set up, demo data). 110 pages and 50 detail views captured. Nothing in the AgencyZoom account was changed.

## What AgencyZoom has, tab by tab

- Dashboard: Sales/Service toggle. Daily goal pacing (started at / should be at / still needed / achieved), month trending vs goal and vs last month, written business chart with time frame, location/agent, carrier and business type filters. Service dashboard: open requests, closed last 30 days, average completion time, service rating, requests by category and policy type, 12-month trend.
- Pipeline: Leads and Opportunities boards. Stages New > Contacted > Quoted > Sold, plus Dead with loss reason. Cards show days old and days inactive. Smart-Cycle (X-date) stage recycles unsold leads. Shot Clock moves aged leads automatically (45 days to Smart-Cycle, recycle after 2 months).
- Lead record: Sold/Dead buttons, stage stepper, age/inactivity counters, primary and other producer, location, tags, multiple contacts, quotes, opportunities, smart-cycle dates, referral, lead source, sequence enrollment. Activity tabs: All, Notes, Deleted Notes, Emails, Text, Task, Call, Files, Video. Text composer with templates, meeting link, scheduling link, auto unsubscribe line. Vehicles and Drivers tabs.
- Customers: list and search. Record has status, contacts, related leads/customers, Policies (carrier, policy number, premium, effective/expiration, lifecycle stage, next step), Activities, Automation, Files, Referrals, Services, Vehicles, Drivers, Welcome Kit button, tags.
- Service Pipeline and Service Center: service requests by category and CSR, unmatched incoming requests from email/Zapier, average completion, survey rating.
- Add Business: personal and commercial (EIN, NAICS, payroll, revenue), broker fee, policy type, carrier, premium, effective dates, producer/CSR split.
- Tasks: Dashboard/List, Past Due Rotting, Past Due Recent, Today, Remainder of week, Next week, Completed; filters by assignee and lead source.
- Lifecycle Automation: per-stage sequences (New: day 1 email + call task + text, day 2 email, day 4 text, and so on), Smart-Cycle day 1/30 emails, Retain sequences (Personal: policy review email 45 days before renewal; Commercial), Service sequences, Events (birthdays, Christmas/New Year email+text, newsletters). Merge fields, send count and open rate per step, business-day scheduling, send time 9 AM EST, holiday exclusions.
- Winbacks: cancelled policy win-back by renewal date.
- Google Reviews: review requests, at-risk customer flags, review management.
- HR, Finance: employee directory, onboarding progress, My Comp, bonus plans, commission rates, payout.
- Reports (about 60): Leads (conversion, funnel, activity, loss reasons, quote status, web lead), New Business (by producer, lead source, policy line, zip, carrier, location, CSR; daily/monthly/annual summaries; mono-line by producer; broker fees), Book of Business, Services, Life & Health, Goals, Activities, Others (email opt-out, batch email). Filter, export, print.
- Settings: Pipelines, Assignment Groups (round-robin), Carriers, Lead Sources, Loss Reasons, Life Products, Custom Fields, Policy Types, Commission Rates, Goals, Service Categories, Tags, Exclusions, Agency/Locations/Staff, Templates (welcome packet, cancellation letter, email), Minimum Coverage, Referral site, meeting link, email integration. System Settings: Lead alerts and visibility, Sales Alerts (new sale push + daily summary email at a chosen hour), Task Manager (assignment emails, today's tasks email, auto-close tasks on sold), Automation (business days, holidays, send time), Work Schedule (hours, timezone), Welcome Packet, Required Data (mandatory fields per object), Unsubscribe Messages, Notifications (per-event toggles, sound), Others (business focus, default auto term 6/12 months, producer/CSR commission split, revenue visibility, auto-create contact from email).
- App Marketplace: web lead integration, Google Reviews, AMS sync (QQ Catalyst, AMS360, EzLynx, HawkSoft, AgencyMatrix, TAM, EPIC, Momentum), raters (PL Rating, Tarmika, TurboRater), Document Intake, Donna, Video, Zapier, Gmail/Outlook, VOIP.

## Where MaxSaveHub is already ahead

- Phone: Telnyx dialer, Live View with whisper/listen/barge, parking lot, transfer. AgencyZoom only links to third-party VOIP.
- Real-time EverQuote intake with dedup, no Zapier needed.
- E-signatures, payments, time clock, payroll, agency P&L, inline texting on the lead card, full vehicle/coverage/driver detail with placeholder VINs.

## Recommended additions

Must have before go-live
1. Automated follow-up sequences per stage (text, email, call task by day; stop on reply, contact or sold; business hours; opt-out).
2. Policy records on the customer (carrier, policy number, type, premium, 6/12 month term, effective/expiration, status, producer/CSR). This is the book of business.
3. Renewal engine: 45-day-prior review touch, renewal list, X-date recycling for unsold leads with an automatic shot clock.
4. Managed lead sources and loss reasons; Dead requires a reason; vendor ROI report (cost vs sold by source).
5. Round-robin assignment groups for incoming vendor leads with an instant alert.
6. Required-data rules and duplicate check on manual add; tags.
7. Email from the lead card with templates and unsubscribe handling.

First 30 days after launch
8. Service requests with categories, CSR assignment, due times and a customer survey.
9. Daily goal pacing on the dashboard and a daily summary email.
10. Winbacks for cancelled policies and Google review requests after a sale.
11. Birthday and holiday touches.
12. Per-policy commission records with producer/CSR split, bonus plans, payout statements.
13. Reports: funnel by source, sales by zip/carrier/policy line, quote status, opt-outs.
14. Per-user notification settings.

Later
15. Commercial intake (EIN, NAICS, payroll, revenue), rater integration, AMS sync, referral program, welcome packet, scheduling link, video messages.
